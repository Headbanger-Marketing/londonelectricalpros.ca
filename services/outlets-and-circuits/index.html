Outlets and dedicated circuits in London.

Countertop appliances in a London kitchen can draw a lot of power at once. In a home you have just bought, the outlets along the counter may look new while the circuits behind them are unknown.

An extra receptacle does not establish extra capacity

Receptacle locations and circuit design answer different questions. More convenient plug-in points may share the same existing circuit. New appliances or specialty equipment may need a different connection, a dedicated circuit or other assessed work. Give the Licensed Electrical Contractor the equipment information and intended location so the proposal follows the actual use.

Prepare the scope for your London property

List the appliances you use on the counter, which ones run at the same time and which outlets they plug into. Record any outlet that has tripped, stopped working or felt loose since you moved in.

Ask how the counter outlets are currently divided between circuits, whether that suits the appliances you listed and what changing it would involve for the backsplash and cabinets.

What should the assessment establish?

A map of which counter outlets share a circuit comes first, and the contractor compares it with the appliances you use together. A past renovation may have replaced the outlets without changing what feeds them, so appearance alone says little. From the map, the contractor explains whether redistributing the outlets, adding a circuit or moving an appliance is the sensible response, and what protection the counter locations need. Which appliances run at once in your London kitchen is for you to say. The written scope names each outlet position and its circuit, with any backsplash or cabinet restoration listed separately.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Why does the microwave trip a breaker when the kettle is also on?

Both appliances may share one circuit that cannot carry them together. Stop running them at the same time, note which breaker trips, and ask the contractor to check how the counter outlets are divided before adding anything new.

Do newer-looking counter outlets mean the kitchen circuits were updated?

Not necessarily. A faceplate or device can be swapped without any change to the circuit behind it. Have the contractor confirm the circuit layout and the condition of the connections rather than judging by appearance.
